Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

The short version: there are no accounts here, we don't ask for your name or email, and your answers stay in your browser. Read on for the details.

1. What we collect 2. Cookies & third parties 3. How we use it 4. Sharing 5. Your choices 6. Your rights 7. Children 8. Security 9. Changes 10. Contact

1. What we collect

We don't collect personal information. There is no sign-up, no login, and no form asking for your name, email address, or date of birth. We do not build profiles about you.

Here is everything that touches data when you use the site:

  • On-device storage. Your test history and counts are saved in your browser's localStorage. This data stays on your device. We never receive it, and clearing your browser data removes it.
  • Shared links. If you copy a challenge link, the name you enter and your answers are encoded into that link. Anyone who has the link can decode them. Only send links to people you're comfortable seeing those answers.
  • Basic server logs. Our hosting provider may record technical information that browsers send automatically — IP address, browser type, pages requested, and timestamps — for security and operation. These logs are not used to identify you personally.

2. Cookies & third parties

We are a static site with no first-party accounts or advertising cookies of our own. However, third-party services we use may set cookies or process data:

  • Cloudflare. Hosts the site and provides security. It may set a minimal cookie to distinguish legitimate traffic from bots.
  • Analytics. If we enable privacy-focused analytics (such as Cloudflare Web Analytics), it collects aggregate page views without cookies or cross-site tracking.
  • Advertising. If we enable Google AdSense, it may use cookies to serve ads and, where permitted, personalize them based on your interests. You can opt out of personalized ads at any time (see Your choices).
  • Social platforms. When you copy a link and share it on WhatsApp, Instagram, or elsewhere, that platform applies its own privacy policy — not ours.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use third-party advertising trackers beyond what is described above.

3. How we use information

Any data we process is used only to:

  • run the site and deliver the test pages you request;
  • understand, in aggregate, which tests people enjoy;
  • keep the site secure and prevent abuse;
  • display advertising, if advertising is enabled.

We do not use your answers to make decisions about you, and we do not send them anywhere.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data. We share information only with the service providers needed to operate the site (hosting and, if enabled, analytics and advertising), and only to the extent required for them to do their jobs. We may also disclose information if legally required to do so.

5. Your choices

  • Clear your data. Delete this site's data in your browser settings to erase locally stored test history.
  • Control cookies. Your browser lets you block or delete cookies. The site still works without them.
  • Opt out of personalized ads. Use Google Ads Settings, or the industry opt-out pages at aboutads.info and youronlinechoices.com. Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals are honored where required.
  • Don't share links. Since answers travel inside the link, simply avoid sharing one if you'd rather keep your answers private.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, and to object to certain processing (including targeted advertising). Under GDPR and CCPA/CPRA, you also have the right not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

Because we collect essentially no personal data, most of these rights have little to act on — but if you have a request, contact us using the address below and we will respond within the time required by law.

7. Children

The site is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. We keep data collection to a minimum, which means there is very little to compromise. No system is perfectly secure, but the design goal here is to hold as little as possible.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site evolves — for example, if we add analytics or advertising. The date at the top will always reflect the latest version. Material changes will be noted on the homepage.

10. Contact

Questions or requests about privacy? Email hello@thetestlibrary.app. We read everything and reply as soon as we can.
